Froggy Happy Jumpy

App: Froggy Happy Jumpy Package: com.preet.froggyhappyjump Developer: Preet Effective Date: November 13, 2026
Overview: Preet ("we", "our", or "us") operates Froggy Happy Jumpy. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. The game is designed to be played and enjoyed without requiring you to create an account, log in, or share personal identifying information with us directly.

A. Local Game Data (Stored on Device Only)

To preserve your game progress, the app saves certain game state information locally on your device in private application storage:

  • High score and highest jungle altitude climbed.
  • In-game currency (Coins earned or purchased).
  • Unlocked and equipped frog character skins.
  • Inventory items (Shield, Jetpack head-starts).
  • User gameplay settings (Sound effects volume, Music toggle, Tilt or Touch steering preferences).

Note: This data is stored strictly on your local device storage. We do not transmit or host your save data on external servers.

B. Device Sensors (Accelerometer & Gyroscope)

If you choose Tilt control mode, the game accesses device accelerometer sensor readings in real-time to allow you to steer your frog left and right across platforms. Sensor readings are processed on-the-fly directly in device memory (RAM) and are never recorded, stored, logged, or transmitted anywhere.

How to Opt Out of Personalized Advertising

You can control personalized advertising or reset your device's advertising identifier at any time via your device settings:

  • On Android: Open Settings → Google → Ads → tap "Delete advertising ID" or "Reset advertising ID".
  • On iOS: Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → toggle off tracking requests.

3. Device Permissions

The app may request the following standard permissions on your device:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required solely to load rewarded video ads from Google AdMob and process Google Play Billing verification.
  • VIBRATE / Haptics: Used to provide subtle tactile feedback during in-game collisions, bounces, and button presses.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Policy)

Froggy Happy Jumpy is a family-friendly vertical jumping game. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or share personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

Third-party advertising SDKs are configured to comply with Google Play Families Policy and COPPA requirements (ad requests can be tagged for child-directed treatment, ensuring non-personalized contextual ads only for younger audiences).

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately at moggapreet9@gmail.com, and we will take prompt action.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your game progress is stored entirely on your local device:

  • You have complete control over your data.
  • To delete all saved game data, you can clear the app's cache and storage via your device's Settings → Apps → Froggy Happy Jumpy → Storage → Clear Data, or simply uninstall the application.

6. Security

We value your trust. While no electronic transmission or storage method is 100% secure, we do not host external user databases, thereby minimizing exposure to data breaches.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA / CPRA)

Depending on your region (such as the European Economic Area or California), you may have rights regarding your data, including:

  • The right to know what personal data is processed.
  • The right to request deletion of any personal data.
  • The right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data (we do not sell your personal data).

To exercise any of these rights, you may manage your ad preferences via your Android device settings or contact us directly.
